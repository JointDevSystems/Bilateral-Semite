About us

Who we are.

Who we are

Quality shipping and freight logistics, from Mombasa.

We strive to offer cost-effective, reliable and efficient logistics solutions to all cadres of clients, with dedicated and multi-user transport solutions and a high priority on integrating logistics operations.

Experience and positioning. Almost twenty years in the sector, covering domestic and international groupage, part- and full-load transport, abnormal and project cargo, customs and inbound and outbound logistics.

Kenya and East Africa focus. Our head office is in Mombasa, the region's gateway port. From there we serve Nairobi, Uganda, Rwanda, the DRC, South Sudan, Tanzania and the wider region.

Our commitment. Reliable, compliant and efficient logistics, delivered by one accountable team.

  • Freight forwarding
  • Customs clearance
  • Container and general cargo transport
  • Abnormal and project cargo
  • Tank-container logistics
  • Warehousing and storage
  • Consolidation & deconsolidation
  • NVOCC, air and sea freight
