About us

Who we are.

What we do

Quality shipping and freight logistics, worldwide.

We strive to offer cost-effective, reliable and efficient logistics solutions to all cadres of clients. We offer a wide choice of dedicated and multi-user transportation solutions and place a high priority on integrating logistics operations.

We have experience in all industry sectors, from domestic and international groupage to part- and full-load transport, and managing inbound and outbound logistics.

  • Consolidation & deconsolidation
  • NVOCC services
  • Air & sea freight
  • Road & rail transport
  • Customs brokerage
  • Warehousing
  • Agency
  • Break bulk, project cargo, FCL and loose cargo
