Who we are

A Mombasa-based logistics company built for East Africa.

Bilateral Semite SAP Ltd is a duly registered shipping and freight logistics company with almost twenty years in the sector. We handle freight forwarding, customs clearance, transport and abnormal and project cargo for clients who need cargo to arrive compliant, safe and on time.

Our focus is Kenya and the wider East African region, supported by branches across the region and agents at the major ports of the world.

  • Reliable: we do what we say we will do
  • Compliant: every move follows customs, port and road rules
  • Efficient: one team coordinating the whole journey

FAQ

Quick answers.

Who is Bilateral Semite SAP Ltd?

Bilateral Semite SAP Ltd is a duly registered shipping and freight logistics agency based in Mombasa, Kenya. We offer cost-effective, reliable and efficient logistics solutions to all cadres of clients, from freight and customs to a secure container depot.

Where are you located?

Our head office is on the 1st Floor, Faiz Ali Building, Nkrumah Rd, Mombasa, Kenya. We have a network of well-established branches across East Africa and agents at all the major ports in the world.

What services do you offer?

We offer LCL consolidation / deconsolidation, NVOCC services, air freight, ocean freight, road and rail transport, clearing and forwarding, warehousing, yard and storage, handling and reefer power, haulage and release, tank containers, bonded warehousing and transhipment. Our freight and logistics services cover consolidation, ocean, air, road and rail, customs and warehousing, while our terminal and depot services cover the yard, handling, release, tank containers, bonded storage and transhipment.

What types of containers do you handle?

Dry, refrigerated and tank containers, including full, empty and specialist units. Tell us your unit type and we will confirm the best handling plan.

Can you power refrigerated containers?

Yes. The terminal has reefer plug points for temperature-controlled units, so cold cargo stays powered while it is in our custody.

How do I get a quote?

Use the form on our contact page or email info@bilateralsemite.com with your unit type, quantity and expected dwell time. We will reply with a clear estimate, and the estimate is free.

How do I arrange a container release?

Send us the unit details and truck information. We check documents and release only to authorised parties.

Can I visit the facility?

Yes. Contact us to arrange a visit to the terminal and head office. Call ahead and we will arrange it.
