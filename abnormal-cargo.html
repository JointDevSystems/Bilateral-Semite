How an abnormal move runs

Eight steps from survey to delivery.

Route surveys, permits, escort coordination, lifting plans, specialized trailers, police and authority coordination, loading and offloading, and delivery supervision, each with a named owner.

  1. Route surveyWe survey the route for bridges, bends, gradients and height limits before committing.
  2. PermitsAbnormal-load and movement permits arranged with the relevant authorities.
  3. Lifting planLifting method and equipment agreed for loading and offloading.
  4. Equipment selectionThe right trailer configuration for the load.
  5. Escort coordinationEscort vehicles arranged and briefed for the route.
  6. Authority coordinationPolice and road authorities informed and coordinated.
  7. Loading & securingSupervised loading, lashing and securing.
  8. Delivery supervisionOffloading supervised on site, with proof of delivery.
