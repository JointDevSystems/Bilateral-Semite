Transhipment

Overview

Built around your cargo.

Transhipment moves cargo from one vessel or vehicle to another on its way to its destination. Our terminal is a secure transfer point where arrivals are planned, units are held safely and releases happen on time.

Reach stackers, reefer power and onward haulage sit on one site, so connections stay short.

  • Short connection times
  • One site, one team
  • Full unit visibility

The process

How it works.

  1. Notice of arrivalYou share carrier and unit details.
  2. Discharge & placementUnits received and stacked.
  3. Hold & monitorSecure custody with regular checks.
  4. Connection call-offYou confirm the onward carrier.
  5. LoadingUnits delivered to the next carrier.
