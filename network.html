Integrated logistics approach

From supplier to empty return, under one plan.

Every consignment follows the same chain. We coordinate each link so nothing is handed off without an owner.

  1. SupplierWe collect cargo details and documents at source.
  2. Origin handlingPick-up, stuffing and export formalities.
  3. Freight forwardingBooking and routing by sea, air or road.
  4. CustomsDeclarations and duty or transit processing.
  5. PortVessel, terminal and release coordination.
  6. TransportationRoad or rail haulage, escorts where needed.
  7. DeliveryOffloading and proof of delivery.
  8. Empty returnContainers returned and closed out.
